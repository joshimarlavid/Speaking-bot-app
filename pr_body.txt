🎯 **What:** The Gemini proxy authentication token (\`lingua-role-secret-token\`) was hardcoded directly in \`server.ts\` and \`src/useLiveAPI.ts\`.
⚠️ **Risk:** Hardcoding secrets inside source code can lead to attackers obtaining the token and gaining unauthorized access to the application's proxy, which communicates directly with the Google Gemini API. This could lead to abuse of proxy endpoints and financial impact.
🛡️ **Solution:** Modified \`server.ts\` to use the \`VITE_PROXY_SECRET_TOKEN\` environment variable (defaulting to the original for backward compatibility in setups where it is not defined) and similarly fetched it from \`import.meta.env\` in \`useLiveAPI.ts\`.
